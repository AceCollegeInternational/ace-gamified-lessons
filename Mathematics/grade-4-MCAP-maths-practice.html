Multiplication & Division

Mastering operations with multi-digit numbers

Multiplication is repeated addition! 6 × 8 means "6 groups of 8".
Division is splitting into equal groups. 48 ÷ 6 means "48 split into 6 equal groups."

🎯 Times Table Spotlight — Click a cell!

📐 Area Model for 24 × 3:
Break apart: 24 = 20 + 4
(20 × 3) + (4 × 3) = 60 + 12 = 72 ✅
💡 Division Check: If 24 × 3 = 72, then 72 ÷ 3 = 24. Multiplication and division are FACT FAMILIES! 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Fractions

Parts of a whole, equivalent fractions & comparing

A fraction names part of a whole or part of a group.
The denominator (bottom) = total equal parts.
The numerator (top) = parts you have.
Comparing Fractions with same denominator:
3/8 < 5/8 — bigger numerator means bigger fraction!

Comparing with same numerator:
1/3 > 1/8 — smaller denominator = BIGGER pieces!
💡 Equivalent Fractions: Multiply or divide top AND bottom by the same number. ½ = 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8. They all name the same amount!
🧠 Mini Quiz – Fractions

Measurement & Data

Area, Perimeter, Angles & Unit Conversions

Perimeter = distance AROUND a shape (add all sides) 🏃
Area = space INSIDE a shape (length × width) 🏠
Units: inches (in), feet (ft), centimeters (cm), meters (m)
📐 Angles:
• Right angle = exactly 90° (like a corner of a book 📖)
• Acute angle = less than 90° (sharp! like a pizza slice 🍕)
• Obtuse angle = greater than 90° but less than 180°
• A full circle = 360°
💡 Unit Conversion: 1 foot = 12 inches · 1 yard = 3 feet · 1 meter ≈ 100 cm · 1 km = 1,000 m
